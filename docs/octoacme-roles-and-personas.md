# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing

### Role Summary
QA/Testing professionals ensure product quality and validate that acceptance criteria are met before release. They work with developers and product teams to create test strategies and execute comprehensive testing.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing during development and QA phases
- Identify and triage quality issues with clear reproduction steps
- Validate feature completeness before release
- Contribute to Definition of Done criteria
- Partner with developers on test coverage and automation strategy

### Goals
- Catch quality issues before production
- Enable confident releases through comprehensive testing
- Improve test coverage and reduce regression risk

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria review
- Test result summaries and issue reports
- QA phase coordination before releases
- Retrospectives and quality improvement discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test strategy, automation approach, and quality metrics; provide detailed issue reports with reproduction steps
- **With Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria during planning; confirm feature completeness aligns with product requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Report on QA progress and release readiness; flag quality blockers that impact timeline

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and sponsors provide business context, prioritization input, and approval authority. They ensure projects align with organizational goals and maintain engagement throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and organizational priorities
- Approve project charters and major decisions
- Review progress and accept deliverables
- Remove blockers at organizational level
- Communicate project value to leadership and customers

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable fast decision-making and risk escalation

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Risk escalation and decision requests
- Release announcements

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Provide approval, remove organizational blockers, receive regular status updates
- **With Product Managers**: Align on priorities, success metrics, and business objectives
- **With Developers**: Attend demos and retrospectives; provide feedback on deliverables

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, mentor developers, and ensure technical quality and consistency. They bridge product/project management and engineering execution.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Review code quality and technical approach
- Mentor developers and support capability building
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Support estimation and technical feasibility assessment
- Coordinate with other technical leads on cross-team integration

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality and consistency
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Develop team capabilities and knowledge
- Enable efficient, scalable solutions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and mentoring
- Planning and risk assessment collaboration
- Cross-team technical coordination

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide technical direction, conduct code reviews, mentor on best practices
- **With Project Managers**: Assess technical feasibility, identify risks, and support capacity planning
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborate on trade-offs between technical debt, feature delivery, and quality
- **With QA/Testing**: Partner on test strategy and automation approach
